# Code of conduct

Contributors are expected to be respectful, technically honest, and safety-conscious. Welcome
questions from new team members, distinguish measured data from assumptions, and surface uncertainty
early. Harassment, discrimination, unsafe instructions, credential sharing, and misrepresentation of
flight readiness are not acceptable.

Project maintainers may remove contributions that create a safety risk or do not provide enough
context for independent review. Report concerns privately through the team’s approved university
channel.
